Sidegrid is a small, single-player city game that runs directly in a browser with no build step or external dependencies. Walk through its street grid, borrow a parked car or a stopped traffic car, and weave around pedestrians and other vehicles.

The city is an open-ended sandbox rather than a campaign. Witnessed theft, striking pedestrians, and punching attract police; getting caught costs a fine. Delivery runs and passenger fares can earn cash, and your position, balance, and completed jobs are saved in the browser.

**Controls**
- **WASD** or **arrow keys** — walk
- **E** — enter or leave a car
- **W / S** — accelerate or reverse while driving
- **A / D** — steer while driving
- **F** — punch a nearby pedestrian
- **P** — pause or resume
- **Enter** — start, continue after a bust, or begin a new game from the title screen
