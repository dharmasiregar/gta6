Sidegrid is a small, dependency-free top-down city game that runs directly in a modern browser. Walk the grid, borrow a parked car, and try to stay ahead of the patrol cars when the city notices your crimes.

Timed delivery and passenger jobs provide cash for your next run. Your position, balance, and completed jobs are saved in the browser; the four staged screenshots can be reproduced with the `?shot=` URLs listed in `shots.json`.

**Controls**
- WASD or arrow keys: walk; while driving, accelerate/reverse and steer
- E: enter or leave a car, start a job, or collect a nearby mission
- F: punch a nearby pedestrian
- P or Escape: pause

Run `node serve.ts` and open `http://localhost:8080` to play.
