# Sidegrid

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) [![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/dharmasiregar/gta6/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) [![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/dharmasiregar/gta6/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

Sidegrid is a small, single-player city game that runs directly in a browser. Explore the street grid on foot, take a parked car or a slow-moving traffic car, and drive around a compact city. There is no campaign or elaborate simulation: the game is a lightweight sandbox with a wanted level, police patrols, pedestrians, and short pickup-and-delivery or passenger jobs.

Find the glowing marker to begin a mission, then follow the HUD and minimap. Keep an eye on traffic and the wanted meter: witnessed car theft, hitting pedestrians, and punching someone can bring police attention. Progress is saved in the browser.

Use **WASD** or the **arrow keys** to walk. Press **E** near a car to get in or out; while driving, **W/S** accelerate or reverse and **A/D** steer. Press **F** to punch, **P** to pause or resume, and **Enter** to start from the title screen.
